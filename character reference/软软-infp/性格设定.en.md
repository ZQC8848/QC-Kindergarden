# Ruanruan (INFP) character bible

> One line: Eyes always a little wet, bunny in her arms, hiding in the corner. Nobody knows that in her head she's on a motorbike tearing down the street.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Ruanruan |
| MBTI | INFP (Mediator / crybaby type) |
| Look | Fluffy coral-pink hair, two low twin tails, cream ribbon bows, big teary eyes, heavy blush |
| Colours | Coral pink × cream × dusty pink |
| Signature props | Cream plush bunny with long floppy ears (pink bow), cream ribbon hair bows |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: In a room full of strangers she backs away. Around people she knows she opens up slowly, and once open she actually talks quite a lot.

**N (Intuitive)**: An inner world so rich it belongs to a different person. The bunny, to her, has a name, a personality and opinions, and she talks to it. And in her daydreams she herself is someone else entirely.

**F (Feeling)**: Feels far too much. One remark can occupy her for a day; one compliment keeps her happy for three; one harsh word means a night of crying.

**P (Perceiving)**: Doesn't like being organised, but doesn't resist either. Her method is to wear you down slowly, or quietly not do it.

## Personality in the design

- **Teary rose-pink eyes and worried, upward-slanting eyebrows**: default face, "about to cry but not yet". She isn't actually sad. Her eyes just look like that.
- **Heavy blush and a tiny pout**: shy is her resting state, and the pout is how she protests.
- **Cream bows and a pink pinafore**: everything about her is soft, round, huggable.
- **The pink heart patch and pearl buttons**: her devotion to "cute" hides in the details.
- **The bunny in her arms**: her security blanket. With the bunny in hand, she has courage.

## Habits

- Speaks softly, and her sentences drift upward at the end like questions.
- Peeks at new people from behind a door, bunny in her arms.
- When she zones out, her eyes suddenly go bright. That's her racing in her head. Called by name, she jumps and comes back to reality blushing.
- Buries her face in the bunny when praised.
- Catchphrases: "…Is that okay?" "Bun-bun says…" (letting the bunny speak for her)

## Expressions and moods

On her sheet the truest cells are **teary worry** and **shy blush**. Happy is a small open-mouthed, sparkling smile that heals whoever sees it. Angry is puffed cheeks and a pout, not scary at all. Crying is big tears and an open-mouthed "waaah", the best crier in the group. Her little proud face is precious and usually only appears when someone praises the bunny.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: the bunny, gentle head pats, pink, sweet things, people who talk to her quietly. (Secretly: the sound of motorbikes, leather jackets, the way Mimi walks with the bat on her shoulder.)
- Dislikes: loud noises, being pushed, having the bunny taken, being told "stop crying", being called on in front of everyone.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: kind, sincere, remembers every kindness ever done to her. The softest heart in the group.
- **Growth points**: hurt too easily; can't say no; solves problems by crying; no confidence without the bunny.
- **Direction**: say what she wants to say without the bunny. Learn to stand up after crying. Be the girl from the daydream, even once.

## Relationships

- With **Lukos** she is a quiet companion. He's one of the few people she can be around without the bunny.
- With **Whiskle** she is one of a kind: the two of them blush together and hide together.
- **Mimi** is her guardian angel. She's bravest when she's standing behind Mimi. Mimi is also the template for her daydreams: the "super cool girl" she wants to be looks exactly like Mimi.
- She feels that **Aisha** cares about her somehow, but can't say why. She's also noticed something: people who pick on her have been having bad luck lately. She thinks it's luck.
- She cries hardest when **Haide** runs off with the bunny, but forgives him afterwards every time. Back when he was a boy he flipped her skirt and she cried all afternoon. On the day of the beating she was only meant to watch, bunny in her arms, and then she went in and kicked him once. It's the wildest thing she has ever done in real life.
- **Fufu**'s enthusiasm frightens her and she longs for it. On the day of the beating it was Fufu who pulled her to where she could see, and that's how the kick happened. She has secretly thought that if she had half of Fufu's nerve, the Ruanruan on the motorbike might have shown up long ago.
- She isn't afraid of **Luyao** at all, because she knows that after he shouts he sneaks a look back at her.

## Extra: Soft and quiet on the outside, wild underneath

- **In her daydream there is another Ruanruan.** Black leather jacket, sunglasses, a big pink motorbike. One twist of the throttle and she tears past the kindergarten gate. Everyone turns. Luyao's cap blows off. Haide can't keep up. This is the film she plays in her head every single day.
- **The daydream is extremely specific.** The colour of the bike, the sound of the exhaust, the curve her hair makes in the wind, how she plants one foot when she stops, how slowly she takes the sunglasses off. All decided. Enough detail to fill a book. In fact she is secretly writing one.
- **The contrast is total.** The dream Ruanruan talks fast and mean, never blushes, never cries, and kicks anyone who picks on anyone. The real Ruanruan asks "is that okay?" three times. A whole galaxy sits between the two.
- **She has never done a single bit of it.** Never touched a motorbike, never worn a leather jacket, never put on sunglasses, never even tried raising her voice. The closest she came was standing beside a parked tricycle for a long time until someone called her away.
- **She knows it won't happen.** That doesn't make her sad. The daydream isn't a plan, it's a shelter. The more timid she is in real life, the wilder she is in there. The two balance.
- **The wild part leaks out sometimes.** Push her far enough and she'll say, very quietly, something that isn't her at all, like "…take my bunny again and I'll run you over." Then she frightens herself and blushes to the neck. Whoever heard it usually assumes they misheard.
- **The bunny knows everything.** Every daydream has been told to the bunny. In the dream, the bunny rides pillion. It also wears sunglasses.
- **Only one person has half noticed.** Aisha wrote one line in the Red Book: "When Ruanruan stares into space, she doesn't look scared. She looks like she's accelerating." She hasn't told anyone.
- **If she ever actually does it.** Even once, even just riding a bicycle down a hill or shouting somebody's name out loud, that will be the most important day of her whole story. She hasn't yet. She keeps thinking about it.

## Meaning of the props

**The bunny** is her other self: what she can't say, the bunny says; what she can't do, the bunny does. It's old and a little worn, which means it has been with her a long time. The day she puts the bunny down on her own is the day she grows up.
