# Fufu (ENFP) character bible

> One line: A small orange that runs and jumps, laughs at absolutely anything, and takes everyone down with her.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Fufu |
| MBTI | ENFP (Campaigner / happy-puppy type) |
| Look | Fluffy tangerine bob, one tall springy cowlick, an orange-slice hair clip |
| Colours | Orange × cream × a touch of leaf green |
| Signature props | Orange-slice hair clip, smiling-orange crossbody bag |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Runs on people. Alone she wilts; drop her into a crowd and she recharges on the spot. She can talk to anyone and makes a friend in five minutes.

**N (Intuitive)**: Ten browser tabs open at once. A cloud becomes a story, every game gets new rules. The downside is that she is usually halfway through one idea when the next one drags her off.

**F (Feeling)**: Decides by whether it feels right. Cares enormously whether other people are happy, and is the first to run over and grab the hand of anyone left standing alone.

**P (Perceiving)**: Hates timetables, loves "let's see". Chronically late, but she apologises with such a grin that nobody can stay annoyed.

## Personality in the design

- **That cowlick on top**: her mood antenna. Perkier when she's happy, droops when she's not.
- **The orange clip and the orange bag**: she thinks of herself as an orange. Sweet, a little sour, and you can peel her and share her round.
- **The huge cream hoodie**: sleeves always too long. It's the "ready to go anywhere" outfit, and it doesn't mind dirt.
- **Big round honey-gradient eyes and permanent blush**: she cannot hide a feeling. Everything is on her face.
- **The wave in her reference image**: that's her default state. Point a camera at her and she says hi.

## Habits

- Talks fast and loud; sentences end in "Eh—!" and "Whoa—!"
- Never walks in a straight line. Hops, spins, stops dead to look at an ant.
- Catchphrases: "I have a GREAT idea!" (three in ten actually are) "Hahahaha wait, let me finish laughing first."
- Names everything, including the classroom broom.

## Expressions and moods

On her expression sheet, **laughing** and **surprised** are the natural ones. Laughing is more than half her day, and she often ends up crouched on the floor slapping her leg. She cries like a fountain and is fine in five minutes. Angry is puffed cheeks, forgotten a minute later. Shy is rare; when it shows up, she really cares.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: anything orange-flavoured, new friends, puddles after rain, being told "you're so much fun".
- Dislikes: queues, sitting still, the word "no", being ignored.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: sincere, infectious, always sure tomorrow will be better. She is the group's mood.
- **Growth points**: three-minute attention span; promises more than she can finish; big swings, one sentence can flatten her.
- **Direction**: finish one idea. Accept that not everyone has to like her.

## Relationships

- With **Haide** she is half of the trouble duo, one comes up with it and the other does it. Back when he was a boy he flipped her skirt, and on the day of the beating she was the one who lured him into the corner. She feels no guilt at all and thinks he's more fun as a dog.
- **Mimi** is the coolest person she knows. In a fight she follows Mimi; at play she follows herself. Mimi is the only one who can stop her with a look, but Fufu is also the only one who dares to tackle-hug Mimi when Mimi is tired. Mimi says "let go" and doesn't push her off.
- She hugs **Aisha** at least once a day. Aisha says "so annoying"; Fufu hears "again". She is the only person who can make Aisha stumble over a word, and she's decided that's Aisha being shy. She's not wrong. She has always assumed the Red Book is a joke collection, because Aisha sometimes smiles at it on her own.
- **Ruanruan** is the one she most wants to hug and least dares to squeeze. She pulls Ruanruan out to play, Ruanruan shrinks back, she pulls again, and if Ruanruan cries she panics more than anyone. On the day of the beating she saw Ruanruan land that kick and thought it was the coolest kick she had ever seen. She still brings it up.
- She drags quiet kids like **Whiskle** into games, and sometimes the enthusiasm scares them.
- The only one in the group who isn't afraid of **Luyao** losing his temper. He shouts, she laughs; he shouts louder, she laughs harder. Her goal is to make him laugh.
- Thinks what **Dianer** says is magical, even though she doesn't understand it.

## Extra: The comedian with no threshold

- **She laughs at everything.** A sneeze, a dog chasing its tail, a mispronounced word, Luyao saying "Plan." with a straight face: all of it leaves her gasping. The group rule is "if you want to know whether something is funny, check whether Fufu laughed. If she didn't, it really isn't."
- **She is funny herself.** She does everyone: Luyao standing straight with his hands behind his back, Aisha's cold face, Dianer pushing his glasses up, Ruanruan clutching the bunny. The impressions are accurate and exaggerated, and the victims are furious and trying not to laugh.
- **She laughs with zero dignity.** Slaps her leg, drops to the floor, laughs till she cries, till she hiccups, till she slides off the chair. She doesn't care. That's what laughing is for.
- **Comedy is how she makes friends.** A new kid won't talk, so she trips over nothing to make them laugh. She believes nobody can be scared while they're laughing.
- **Her laugh is contagious.** One person laughing is nothing, but when Fufu laughs the people next to her go, and in the end even the corner of Luyao's mouth twitches. That is her real job in this group.
- **She knows when not to.** Low threshold, good judgement. When someone is genuinely sad she goes quiet, waits a while, then asks softly, "Want me to tell you a joke?"
- **Her dream targets are Luyao and Aisha**, because they're the hardest. She has succeeded a few times and counts each one as the achievement of the year.
- **Her intelligence comes from Mushi.** Before she tries to make someone laugh, she asks Mushi how that person is doing today. Whatever she asks, he answers, and she has never had to ask twice.
- **New target: the transfer student Liiie.** A hundred methods tried, success rate zero. Liiie smiles every time and says "Fufu, you're so funny", and that's the end of it. Fufu doesn't know Liiie is laughing her head off inside.

## Meaning of the props

**The smiling-orange bag** holds surprises, not things: sweets, coloured stones, drawings for friends. She believes that at any moment she can pull something out of it and make someone smile.
