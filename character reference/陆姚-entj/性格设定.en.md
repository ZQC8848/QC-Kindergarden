# Luyao (ENTJ) character bible

> One line: Cap on, he's the commander. Cap off, he's a powder keg. Worst temper and foulest mouth in the group.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Luyao |
| MBTI | ENTJ (Commander / little-general type) |
| Look | Short black hair, officer's cap, double-breasted black greatcoat, burgundy tie, a serious face |
| Colours | Black × burgundy × silver |
| Signature props | Black peaked cap (silver diamond badge), burgundy tie |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Born to stand at the front. Not for the spotlight; because "someone has to be in charge".

**N (Intuitive)**: Sees the big picture. While the others debate what to play today, he's thinking about what this term should achieve.

**T (Thinking)**: Direct, efficient, no detours. Extremely demanding of himself, exactly as demanding of everyone else. When someone falls short of his standard he doesn't "seem angry", he blows.

**J (Judging)**: Plan, execute, check, review. The word "whatever" does not exist in his world.

## Personality in the design

- **The cap pulled low and the silver badge**: the cap is his authority. The straighter it sits, the more serious he is.
- **Straight eyebrows and a pressed mouth**: default face, "I'm waiting for your report".
- **Double-breasted coat, epaulettes, cross-belt**: order, head to toe. Not one button is done up casually.
- **The burgundy tie and the coat lining**: heat hidden under the black. He isn't cold; he just doesn't show it.
- **The "cap off" view that appeared by accident in the reference set**: hair a little messy, eyes a little softer. That's what he looks like when he isn't commanding anyone.

## Habits

- Short, fast, forceful sentences. Fond of "now", "immediately", "as planned".
- Lines people up, splits them into groups, sets timetables, and actually enforces them.
- Stands ramrod straight, hands behind his back.
- Catchphrases: "Don't make me swear at you." "Plan." "No. Again." "I'll take responsibility." Plus assorted words that can't go on a kindergarten wall.

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the truest cells are the **stern level stare** and **lowered-brow displeasure**. Surprised, his eyes go wide but his mouth stays shut. Angry is the most explosive in the group: brows all the way down, eyes round, mouth open and swearing, zero to a hundred in a second. Only at the very peak does he go quiet instead, and that's when he's most frightening. A real laugh is extremely rare and turns him into a different person. Crying is never to be seen; he pulls the cap down first. Shy is a straight face and red ears.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: a plan carried out perfectly, being treated as an adult, neat lines, winning.
- Dislikes: delays, excuses, mess, people who question his decision without a better one.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: decisive, takes responsibility, gets more done than anyone. The group's engine.
- **Growth points**: too hard; doesn't understand that some things don't need efficiency; commands instead of talking; won't allow himself a weak moment; a hair trigger, and no apology afterwards.
- **Direction**: ask "how do you want to do this?" Take the cap off now and then. Count to three before swearing.

## Relationships

- **Aisha** is the puzzle he can't solve: why is this person never rattled? He blows up and she's turning a page. The whole group is in chaos and she's turning a page. He shouts at her and she glances up, then back down to the page. They hardly ever work together, but he's secretly learning from her: he tried counting silently to three before exploding, and blew at two. He thinks the Red Book is a battle plan. He asked to borrow it, was refused, and has been even more certain since.
- He and **Mimi** are the two captains. He can blow up at anyone in the group except her. Not for lack of wanting; it just doesn't pay: her bat against his mouth would have no winner. Most of the time it's a quiet contest, each on their own ground, neither stepping on the other's line. Openly, they're civil: nod in passing, opposite sides of the table in meetings. When something real happens they work together without a word, and afterwards go back to keeping a wary eye on each other.
- **Haide** baits him every single day. He answers every line seriously, then blows, then chases him round the courtyard swearing. Haide thinks this is the best game in the world.
- He has no answer to **Fufu**'s nonsense, because she doesn't follow any logic at all.
- He tells **Ruanruan** to "stand up straight" in his harshest voice, then stands behind her and blocks everyone else.
- The person he hates most is the Chairman, **QC**. He and Mimi actually run this kindergarten, while its owner, in that Hawaiian shirt, shows up a few times a year to sign, refill and leave with a companion. Luyao once brought him the budget; QC looked at it for three seconds, signed, and said "you're doing great". Luyao's cap went crooked with rage. Every meeting he blows, and every time QC hands him an orange juice.

## Extra: Hot-tempered, aggressive, and swears a lot

- **A very short fuse.** Delays, excuses, sloppy work, or simply not standing straight enough: any of it sets him off instantly. There is no transition from calm to fury. One second it's "Plan.", the next he's shouting.
- **Swearing is his first reaction, not his last.** He doesn't build up to it; he opens with it. "Damn", "get lost" and "you f—" are everyday vocabulary, and at full temper it becomes a string nobody can make out. He thinks this is perfectly normal. "I didn't hit anyone."
- **The aggression is for everyone.** No exceptions, no occasion. He swears at Haide, swears at Mimi, swears at Mushi. The one exception is Ruanruan: he swore at her once, she cried all afternoon, and ever since he clenches his jaw before he speaks to her.
- **No apologies.** He thinks everything he said was right; it was just "a bit direct". Once he's cooled down, instead of sorry, you get "I've reassigned the tasks" or "I saved you the good spot".
- **The aggression is also how he protects.** If someone picks on one of his own, he's first in, swearing harder and standing further forward than anyone. Mimi deters with the bat; he does it with his mouth and his fists.
- **The more he cares, the faster he blows.** People he doesn't care about, he can't be bothered to swear at. The ones he shouts at hardest are exactly the people and the things that matter most to him. If Aisha's plan goes wrong he shouts at Aisha; if his own does, he shouts at himself.
- **The cap is his switch.** With it on, he tries to control himself, badly. With it off, no control at all. So everyone has learned: when he pulls the cap down, he's about to blow. Scatter.
- **Fufu is the only one who isn't afraid.** He shouts, she laughs; he shouts louder, she laughs harder. He has nothing on her, and that's why she occasionally gets a laugh out of him.
- **He knows it's a problem.** Alone, he practises "talking nicely" in the mirror. Three minutes in, he's swearing at the mirror.

## Meaning of the props

**The cap** is his identity: put it on and he's the one responsible. He rarely takes it off, because without it he doesn't know who he is. **The burgundy tie** is his soft spot, the only warm colour in the black uniform, the gentleness he won't admit but has never lost. After he's shouted at someone he tugs the tie without noticing. That's regret. He just doesn't say so.
