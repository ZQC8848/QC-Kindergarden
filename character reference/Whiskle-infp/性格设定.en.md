# Whiskle (INFP) character bible

> One line: Far more at ease putting a seed in the soil than talking to people. Also, for the record, he's a boy.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Whiskle |
| MBTI | INFP (Mediator / little-gardener type) |
| Gender | Boy (though almost everyone's first guess is girl) |
| Look | Long sage-green hair, a braid over one shoulder, a brass leaf clip, a leather satchel with pencils sticking out, a face that is far too pretty |
| Colours | Olive × moss × brown × cream |
| Signature props | Terracotta pot with a green sprout, dark green hardback journal (gold leaf emblem), brown leather satchel with pencils |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: When the world gets too loud he goes and stands next to a plant. Plants don't talk, but they grow, and that calms him.

**N (Intuitive)**: There is an entire imaginary forest in his head. He keeps a journal, draws, and gives every plant a story.

**F (Feeling)**: Values as firm as rock, just never spoken. You can get him to do a great deal, but not anything he thinks is wrong.

**P (Perceiving)**: Slow tempo, takes things as they come. He doesn't hurry a seed and he doesn't hurry himself.

## Personality in the design

- **The long braid over his shoulder and the cream ribbon**: soft, quiet, a little old-fashioned. Also the number-one reason people take him for a girl. He has no plans to cut it, because "the braid isn't in the way".
- **A face that's too fine**: long lashes, round cheeks, gentle eyes. The group agrees it's the prettiest face here, prettier than several of the girls.
- **Moss-green eyes with a gold gradient**: when he looks at something, he really looks, the way you'd study a leaf's veins.
- **Chunky olive cardigan with wooden buttons**: every material natural, warm, breathing.
- **The pencils in the satchel**: always ready to draw what he sees. Doesn't matter if it's good; it's for himself.
- **The restrained smile in his reference image**: his happiness folds inward. It doesn't spill.

## Habits

- Speaks softly and often stops halfway to find the word.
- Steps around insects on the path.
- Always has a seed or a leaf in his pocket.
- Catchphrases: "I really am a boy." "It's growing." "I think… maybe… mm."

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the truest cells are **shy** and **lost in thought**. Surprised, his eyes go very wide, like a startled fawn. Angry is almost invisible: he goes quiet and walks away. Crying is quiet too, alone, facing a flowerpot. Proud is very rare, probably reserved for the day a seed sprouts.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: the smell of soil, rain, sprouting, a fresh page in the journal, company that stays quiet.
- Dislikes: being hurried, being shouted at, seeing a plant trodden on, being made to "perform", explaining "I'm a boy" for the hundredth time.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: kind, patient, has a world of his own. The least calculating person in the group.
- **Growth points**: avoids conflict; thinks more than he does; when misunderstood he doesn't explain, he just holds it in.
- **Direction**: say "I don't like this". Let someone see one page of the journal.

## Relationships

- He and **Ruanruan** are the same species; put them together and they blush in unison.
- He gets on best with **Dianer**: one grows plants, one plays games, each doing their own thing side by side. Dianer was the first person to call him a boy at first sight, and he has never forgotten it.
- When **Fufu** drags him off to play he panics, then secretly enjoys it afterwards.
- He rather admires **Lukos**, and thinks of him as "the version of me that looks after people".

## Extra: A boy, but too pretty, and everyone gets it wrong at first

- **First impression, every time: "what a pretty little girl."** Long hair, braid, ribbon, gentle eyes, soft voice. Nothing about him says boy. Ten out of ten newcomers get it wrong.
- **He corrects them very quietly.** "…I'm a boy." So quietly the other person often doesn't hear, and goes on treating him as a girl. He isn't angry, just a little helpless, and then he lets it go.
- **Everyone has got it wrong.** On day one Fufu grabbed him with "us girls should play together". Mimi put him on her list of girls to protect. The first time Luyao shouted at him he used the words "little miss". Even Aisha wrote the wrong gender in the Red Book and quietly corrected it later. Haide was the only one who smelled the truth from the start, but he thought watching everyone get it wrong was funnier, so he said nothing.
- **He has never wanted to change.** People have suggested cutting the hair, losing the braid. He shakes his head. This is who he is; being pretty isn't wrong, and neither is liking plants and ribbons. This is the most immovable part of his INFP: people can say what they like, but he won't change for them.
- **Being pretty brings him trouble, not pride.** Wrong names, second looks, whispers. He doesn't like any of it. He'd rather people looked at his flowerpot than his face.
- **When someone gets it right, he lights up.** If someone says "hi, you're a boy, right?" on first sight, he freezes for a second and then produces the rarest thing in the group: a genuinely happy smile. So far only Haide and Dianer have earned it.
- **Standing next to Ruanruan makes it worse.** Both soft, both pink-and-green, both looking at the floor. People call them "those two little girls". Ruanruan blushes; he says, very quietly, "…one."

## Meaning of the props

**The sprout in the pot** is his idea of time: good things come slowly. **The journal** is his entire inner life; whoever gets to open it is the person he trusts most. **The pencils** mean he is always recording. To him, this world is worth looking at closely.
