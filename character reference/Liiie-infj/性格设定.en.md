# Liiie (INFJ) character bible

> One line: By day a perfectly mannered rich girl, by night a gamer who doesn't sleep. Both are real; they just never appear at the same time.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Liiie |
| MBTI | INFJ (Advocate / young-lady type) |
| Status | Transfer student, second year |
| Look | Raspberry-red curls past the knees, a rose-and-pearl hair ornament, a burgundy Lolita dress, a lace capelet, little heels |
| Colours | Burgundy × raspberry × cream × pearl |
| Signature props | Rose-and-pearl hair ornament, burgundy handbag on a pearl chain, a burgundy bow at the back of her head |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: Socialising costs battery. She's impeccable in company, but being impeccable is itself the cost. Home, door shut, game on: that's the charger.

**N (Intuitive)**: Reads people accurately. Who's being polite and who means it, she can tell at a glance. She says a lot of polite things herself, and she knows they're polite things.

**F (Feeling)**: Cares how people feel, so she's courteous to everyone. But courtesy and closeness are two different things, and she draws that line very clearly.

**J (Judging)**: On the surface her life is orderly: neat clothes, careful speech, homework on time. The order just doesn't extend to her sleep schedule.

## Personality in the design

- **Curls past the knees, not one out of place**: a long time in front of the mirror every morning. Two hours of sleep or not, the hair will be perfect. That's her line.
- **The rose-and-pearl ornament and the bow at the back**: delicate, expensive, obviously "from a good family". She doesn't show off, but she doesn't hide it either.
- **Lace high collar and capelet**: wrapped up completely, not an inch of skin. The sense of distance starts here.
- **The finger-to-the-lips pose in her reference image**: her signature gesture. It looks like thinking. It's suppressing a yawn.
- **Slightly drooping eyebrows and a closed mouth**: default face, "gentle but not awake". People take it for a young lady's reserve. It's sleep deprivation.
- **The little heels**: slow and steady, the way a lady walks. Also the way someone who hasn't slept walks.

## Habits

- Always speaks formally, with soft little tags at the end of sentences. Even to Luyao: "Luyao, please don't swear in the classroom, okay?"
- Smiles and nods to everyone she meets, Haide included.
- Never volunteers anything about herself. Asked what she did last night: "I went to bed early."
- Glassy-eyed in first period; when called on, she answers "Yes?" half a beat late.
- Catchphrases: "Of course, no problem." "Please take care of me." "…Sorry, I'm not feeling well today."

## Extra one: Polite to everyone, and somehow always at a distance

- **Her courtesy makes no distinctions.** Teachers, Mimi, Ruanruan, Haide at his worst: the same smile, the same formal register. It puts people at ease and gives them nothing to hold on to.
- **The distance comes from never saying one word more.** She answers every question and never adds anything. Ask her favourite colour: "Red." And that's it. You don't learn why, or what else she likes.
- **What people feel is: she's lovely, and there's always a pane of glass.** Fufu has tried a hundred ways to make her laugh out loud, success rate zero. Mushi thinks "there's a door in there somewhere" and can't find the handle.
- **The distance is camouflage.** A transfer student doesn't want trouble; a rich girl doesn't want special treatment; a gamer doesn't want to be found out. Three layers of reasons, and that's the glass.
- **Occasionally something real leaks.** Someone mentions a game's hidden level in class and her eyes flash, then the smile is back: "Oh, what's that?"

## Extra two: Secretly an all-night gamer

- **Every night at nine she "goes to sleep", which means she turns the game on.** All-nighters aren't rare, and on the morning after ranking night she especially can't get up.
- **Competitive, puzzle, life-sim: she plays all of them, and well.** In-game she is far fiercer than in real life, and almost nobody would connect the two.
- **She sometimes fakes being ill to stay home.** The excuse is always polite and believable; the day off is spent sleeping till noon and then playing on.
- **Her grades are inexplicably good.** She's very good at spotting patterns and priorities, and never admits the late nights affect her.

> Her Kings of Rant rank, ID, and the secret she shares with Dianer and Haide are now the memory event "Kings of Rant: Ranking Night".

## Extra three: The rumoured fan club

- **At her previous kindergarten she supposedly had an enormous fan club.** The rumours vary: dozens of members, the whole school, a branch at the kindergarten next door.
- **The details are equally wild.** People queued at the gate every morning to say hello; someone found a hair tie she dropped and had it framed; a crowd of kids ran crying after the bus the day she left.
- **Her response: a smile, and "everyone was just being kind".** Neither confirmed nor denied.
- **The truth is probably in between.** She really is popular: pretty, well-off, nice to everyone, never makes an enemy. Someone gentle with everybody naturally gets liked by a lot of people. The fan club probably existed, just smaller and saner than the stories.
- **Why she really transferred, nobody knows.** Some guess the fan club got too much, some guess a family decision. She only says "we moved". Aisha wrote in the Red Book: "Stated reason for transfer does not hold. Under observation."
- **A new fan club is already sprouting here.** A few kids have started waiting at the gate for her in the mornings. She smiles and nods at each of them, then goes back to the classroom to be sleepy.

## Expressions and moods

On her sheet the truest cells are the **gentle smile** and **sleepy**. Sleepy is the face she wears most in a day; it's just usually covered by politeness. A real laugh is rare, and if it appears she genuinely couldn't hold it. Angry is eyebrows slightly down with the smile unchanged, which is worse. Crying is quiet and only when she's alone. The blush appears when someone touches on games. The proud little smile has appeared exactly once, at dawn on the morning her rank went up, and nobody saw it.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: games (every kind), the quiet at three in the morning, roses, being treated as an ordinary classmate, holidays.
- Dislikes: getting up early, questions about her private life, special treatment, being called "young lady", anyone saying she "looks like she has nothing to do".

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: beautifully brought up, sharp-eyed, never causes trouble, learns astonishingly fast. The lowest-maintenance person in the group.
- **Growth points**: a sleep schedule in ruins; keeps everyone out with politeness; the fake sick days will be found out eventually; nobody she can tell the truth to.
- **Direction**: tell one person "actually I was gaming till four last night". Open the glass a crack.

## Relationships

- Most courteous of all to **Mimi**, because Mimi is the captain. Mimi wanted to put her on the list of girls to protect; she smiled and said "there's no need."
- **Dianer** is the only person who might know her secret, and her rival for second place in Kings of Rant for the past six months (she's ninety percent sure). They have never discussed games, but occasionally their eyes meet with an odd understanding. What she doesn't know is that Dianer also knows something about first place, and will never tell her.
- **Aisha** has a page on her in the Red Book, headed "Under observation". She knows Aisha is watching her, and Aisha knows she knows. Polite and wary, both ways.
- **Fufu** has made it her life's mission to make Liiie laugh, and hasn't managed yet. Liiie actually finds Fufu hilarious; she just laughs on the inside.
- **Mushi** is one of the few people she'll give an extra sentence to, because when he asks "are you tired today?" he genuinely wants to know.
- Completely immune to **Haide**'s mischief. He can't flip her skirt (three layers of petticoat), can't run off with her bag (the pearl chain is wound round her wrist), and when he tries to bait her she just smiles. He thinks she's the hardest person in the group to deal with.
- **Ruanruan** thinks she looks like a princess from a fairy tale and doesn't dare go near. Liiie's courtesy has a little more warmth in it for Ruanruan than for anyone else, because Ruanruan reminds her of herself when she was small.
- Exactly the same courtesy for the Chairman, **QC**, as for everyone else, not a shade more. QC finds that "interesting". They're opposites in style, one wrapped up to the chin, one with his shirt open, but on the point of "keeping yourself under a layer", they're the same kind, and they can both tell.

## Meaning of the props

**The rose-and-pearl ornament** is her armour: with it on she's the young lady, with it off she's the gamer. **The handbag** holds a handkerchief and a small mirror on top and a handheld console in the lining, never once taken out at school. **The bow at the back of her head** is the one thing she can't see herself, and the only part of her whole look that isn't for anyone else.
