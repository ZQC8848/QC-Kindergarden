# Mimi (ENFJ) character bible

> One line: The kid with the baseball bat. Someone gets picked on, she's the first one in.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Mimi |
| MBTI | ENFJ (Protagonist / big-sister type) |
| Look | Deep violet spiky hair, high side ponytail, star hair clip, a confident sideways smirk |
| Colours | Purple × black × white |
| Signature props | Silver baseball bat (purple grip and stripes), silver star hair clip |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Born in the middle of the room. She doesn't try to be the centre; people just look at her.

**N (Intuitive)**: Sees what this group could become. She expects a lot from people because she genuinely believes you can do it.

**F (Feeling)**: Fiercely protective. If one of her own gets hurt she remembers it for a year. Tough on the outside, but the best carer in the group, just in a very direct way.

**J (Judging)**: Says it, does it, hates dithering. Hands out jobs and actually checks.

## Personality in the design

- **Narrowed purple eyes and a small proud smile**: default expression, "I've got you all."
- **Hair like a hedgehog**: energy pushing outwards. The side ponytail is her one girly touch, tied high, still sharp.
- **Open varsity jacket over a black hoodie**: sporty, ready for a fight at any moment. The white star on her chest is her badge. It means "captain".
- **Mismatched socks**: on purpose. She makes the rules, so she can break them.
- **The baseball bat**: she says it's for baseball. Nobody has ever seen her play baseball.

## Habits

- Speaks in commands: "Go." "Don't cry." "With me."
- Walks like a gust, bat over her shoulder.
- Quietly saves the best spot for the smallest kid.
- Catchphrases: "I've got you." "Who's picking on you?"

## Expressions and moods

On her sheet the truest cells are the **confident smirk** and the **angry frown**. When she really laughs a completely different person shows up, an ordinary kid. Shy means hiding behind the bat. She almost never cries; when she does she turns away first so nobody sees.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: leading, winning, being trusted, watching her people get stronger.
- Dislikes: bullies, people who don't keep their word, anyone who says "you, a girl…".

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: takes responsibility, acts fast, a natural leader. The group's spine.
- **Growth points**: controlling; decides things for people "for their own good"; won't let herself look weak.
- **Direction**: ask "what do you want?" before handing out the answer. Let someone protect her for once.

## Relationships

- She and **Mushi** are both ENFJs with completely different styles: she runs on presence, he runs on warmth. They sometimes argue over who's in charge, but together they're unstoppable.
- She is **Ruanruan**'s guardian. Anyone who makes Ruanruan cry has to get past her.
- **Fufu** is her front line. On the day of the beating, Fufu was the one who led Haide into the corner; Mimi only had to be waiting there. Fufu's noise gives her a headache, but she has never really been angry with her, because when Mimi's face is set, Fufu is the only one who dares to make her laugh, and it always works.
- **Aisha** is her strategist. They never make small talk, but in a fight nobody is more in sync: Aisha writes the plan, Mimi swings. That is how Case 001 happened. Mimi never asks why. Aisha's plans have never failed, and Mimi doesn't need to understand chess, only where to hit. Neither of them has ever said it, but both treat Ruanruan as their responsibility, one blocking in front, one calculating behind.
- She and **Luyao** are the two captains, and the kindergarten is really run by the pair of them. Most of the time it's a quiet contest: he handles discipline, she handles people, whoever's ground it is has the final say, and a crossed line gets a look. Almost never open conflict, because both know the other is not to be messed with and neither wants to test it. Nod in passing, sit on opposite sides in meetings, keep the peace and love.
- Her verdict on the Chairman, **QC**, is "useless but harmless". He can't handle a crying Ruanruan and always ends with "this one's yours", handed to her. Her reply is always "just don't make it worse."
- She has the lowest tolerance for **Haide**'s mischief and cleans up after him more than anyone. When he was a boy and flipped her skirt, she led the beating and made him swear "do it again and you turn into a dog". When he actually turned into one, her first reaction was "I told you." The collar and the name tag were her doing.

## Meaning of the props

**The bat** has never actually hit anyone. It is a statement: I can protect you. On her shoulder it's a deterrent; put down, it's trust. **The star clip** is her captain's badge. Stars on the jacket, the socks, the shoes. She sees herself as everyone's star.
