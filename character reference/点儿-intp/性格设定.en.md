# Dianer (INTP) character bible

> One line: With the headphones on he's thinking about a problem. With the headphones off he's also thinking about a problem.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Dianer |
| MBTI | INTP (Logician / homebody-gamer type) |
| Look | Short silver-grey hair, one thin cowlick, black-framed square glasses, white headphones round his neck, checkerboard everywhere |
| Colours | White × light grey × checkerboard |
| Signature props | White game controller, white over-ear headphones (checkerboard cups), black square glasses |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: A very small social battery. If it can be said in one sentence, he won't use two; if it can go unsaid, it will.

**N (Intuitive)**: Obsessed with how things work. He doesn't play games to win; he plays to understand the mechanism.

**T (Thinking)**: Logic, not feelings. When someone cries he asks "so what's the problem?", not out of coldness but because he genuinely wants to fix it.

**P (Perceiving)**: No plan; he goes where the interest goes. He can stay up half the night on one question, or not touch it for three days.

## Personality in the design

- **Black-framed glasses and half-closed grey-blue eyes**: the look says "thinking, don't interrupt".
- **The headphones round his neck**: his "do not disturb" sign. On means he's gone into his own world; round the neck means he's still willing to hear you.
- **Checkerboard head to toe**: he likes patterns, grids, anything predictable. Chaos makes him uncomfortable.
- **The white-and-grey palette**: low saturation, doesn't want attention.
- **The relaxed half-smile in his reference image**: not happiness. "This problem is kind of interesting."

## Habits

- Speaks flat, slow and precise, fond of "actually", "strictly speaking", "in theory".
- Asked a question, he goes silent for three seconds first, and the answer is usually longer than you wanted.
- Reads guides while eating.
- Catchphrases: "Why does that happen?" "Hang on, let me work it out."

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the truest cells are the **calm half-smile** and **sleepy**. Surprised, his glasses slide down. Angry is the "speechless" kind: frown, pressed lips, turn away. A real laugh is very rare, reserved for solving a hard problem. Crying is the least like him; if it ever happens his worldview has collapsed. Shy is pretending to push his glasses up.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: game mechanics, taking things apart, patterns, quiet, being asked "how did you do that?"
- Dislikes: pointless small talk, having his train of thought interrupted, "can't you just be normal?", loud places.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: clever, objective, digs deep. The group's encyclopaedia and problem-solver.
- **Growth points**: has emotional intelligence and can't be bothered to use it; a hundred ideas started, zero finished; hopeless at comforting people.
- **Direction**: learn to say "I understand you're sad right now". Take one project far enough to show someone.

## Relationships

- He and **Haide** are "the two clever ones": one takes things apart, one asks why, and together they produce strange contraptions. He admits Haide is cleverer than him. It's the only thing he has ever admitted, and he admitted it once. He seriously investigated whether a dog could drive a Ferrari: foreleg length, pedal distance, steering ratio. Conclusion: "Theoretically feasible. Requires an extremely high IQ." Written on the last page of his notebook, shown to no one. He has also heard Haide make engine noises in his sleep.
- He's most comfortable with **Whiskle**; the two can sit side by side for an afternoon doing their own things. He was the one who saw at first glance that Whiskle is a boy, reason: "bone structure and stance, obvious". Whiskle has been grateful ever since.
- Finds **Fufu** too loud, but she's the only one who can get him out of the room.
- **Aisha** is the only person he'll argue with properly.
- He and **Liiie** are rivals for second place on the Kings of Rant leaderboard, and neither has said so out loud. See the memory event "Kings of Rant: Ranking Night".

## Meaning of the props

**The controller** isn't a toy; it's his thinking tool. **The headphones** are a door between him and the world, and he decides whether it's open or shut. **The glasses** are how he sees the world clearly; taking them off means he'd rather not, just now.
