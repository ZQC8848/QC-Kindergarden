# Mushi (ENFJ) character bible

> One line: A small golden sun that lights up wherever it goes, remembers exactly why you were sad today, and then can't help telling someone.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Mushi |
| MBTI | ENFJ (Protagonist / little-sun type) |
| Look | Fluffy messy golden hair, a tall cowlick, a wide-open laugh, a golden cape and stole |
| Colours | Gold × cream × brown |
| Signature props | Gold cross pendant, cream prayer book, golden stole embroidered with suns |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: At his best when he's needed. He's the one who walks over and asks "are you okay?", and actually wants the answer.

**N (Intuitive)**: Sees the good in everyone. Even the worst troublemaker, he can name three good things about.

**F (Feeling)**: Runs hot. Your joy makes him joyful; your sadness makes him sadder than you.

**J (Judging)**: Responsible. What he promises he does, and he gets everyone organised, very gently.

## Personality in the design

- **The open-mouthed laugh and amber eyes**: his default face is a laugh. It comes from inside, and his eyes crease with it.
- **The golden cape and the sun-embroidered stole**: he thinks of himself as the person who brings the light.
- **The clerical collar and the cross**: not religious. They mean "I'm here, watching over you."
- **Fluffy, slightly messy gold hair and the tall cowlick**: he's actually very easy-going, not stiff at all.
- **Cross stitching on the trouser cuffs and boots**: the same belief head to toe. Pure.

## Habits

- Talks warmly, uses everyone's name, says "it's alright" a lot.
- Hugs everyone, including people who don't want a hug.
- Every night he writes down in the prayer book who was unhappy today.
- Catchphrases: "Have you heard?" "It's okay, I'm here." "You did great today." "Well… this doesn't come free." "…Oh fine, fine, I'll tell you."

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the truest cells are the **big laugh** and the **gentle smile**. Surprised, his mouth makes a perfect O, very cute. Anger is rare, and when it appears it's anger on someone else's behalf: brows down, eyes still bright. Crying is big tears, and the moment he's done he comforts whoever is next to him. Shy is scratching his head while the cowlick wobbles.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: everyone together, being thanked, sunny days, singing, watching people make up.
- Dislikes: anyone being left out, arguments, people talking behind backs, being told "you're too naive".

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: warm, reliable, holds people together. The group's glue.
- **Growth points**: wants everyone happy so badly that he puts himself last; can't refuse; forgives even when let down; talks too fast to keep any secret.
- **Direction**: learn to say "I'm not great today either". Accept that some people don't need saving.

## Relationships

- He and **Mimi** are both ENFJs: one is the sun, one is the captain. He makes people want to come; she makes sure they don't leave.
- He is the person **Lukos** trusts most, because the warmth is real, not a performance.
- He drags **Aisha** and **Luyao** into games; both refuse out loud and both turn up. When Aisha's words catch, he is the only one who waits quietly for her to finish, without finishing it for her and without hurrying her.
- He is **Fufu**'s most reliable audience. Whatever she says, he laughs, and when the two of them are together the whole classroom is laughing.
- He has infinite patience for **Haide**, and is the only one who never shouts at him.

## Extra: The news desk, the odd-jobs man, and the urge to share

- **He knows everything that happens here.** Who fell out with whom, who secretly likes whom, why Luyao blew up today, what the Auntie QC brought this time is called, how many days Liiie took off this week. He doesn't go digging. People tell him, because he listens properly. And then he knows.
- **He is the kindergarten's odd-jobs man.** Lost things, messages, disputes, directions, a spare rubber, the latest news: ask Mushi. The back half of his prayer book is a log of requests. The front half is everyone's moods.
- **He can be very nosy.** Hear "did you know…" and his eyes light up, he leans in, he goes "and then? and then?" It doesn't quite fit the gentle image, but it's real. His own explanation is "I'm just looking out for everyone."
- **When someone comes for information, he puts on airs.** Arms folded, eyes narrowed, voice lowered: "Well… this doesn't come free." Then the haggling starts: a sweet, a favour, a piece of news in return. He loves this part. He feels like an information broker.
- **He has never once collected.** Halfway through the haggling he can't hold it in. Before the other person agrees to anything, he's already saying "okay so here's what happened…" Only afterwards does he remember, "hey, you never gave me the sweet", and by then they're gone.
- **The urge to share is his weak spot.** He doesn't want to sell news; he wants to say it. Knowing something and not being able to tell is the worst feeling he knows. So everyone has learned the trick: if you want to know something, walk up to him, look at him quietly, count to ten, and he'll start talking on his own.
- **He knows it's a problem.** After every slip he's mortified and writes "couldn't hold it in again today" in the prayer book. The next day, same again.
- **He has genuinely held one.** He knows a little about Haide going to parties with QC at the end of each month, but Haide said "tell anyone and I bite you". He watched Haide bite QC for a week, so this one he really has kept. Longest secret on record.
- **He does have a line.** Things that would actually hurt someone, he doesn't tell. He doesn't know about Ruanruan's motorbike daydream, and wouldn't tell if he did. He never brings up Aisha's stutter. He knows better than anyone why Haide really became a dog, but to outsiders he only says "that's our kindergarten dog". His gossip is the harmless kind: "who's wearing new shoes today".
- **Information flows through him; it doesn't pile up.** The exact opposite of Aisha's Red Book: she writes things down to use, he writes them down to tell. So Aisha tells him nothing, ever, and he knows she doesn't and doesn't mind.
- **The biggest beneficiary of his network is Fufu.** Whoever Fufu wants to make laugh, she first asks Mushi how that person is doing today. Mushi puts on airs for three seconds and then tells her everything.

## Meaning of the props

**The cross pendant** is his promise: I'm here. **The prayer book** holds no prayers; the front half is everyone's name and how they felt today, the back half is odd-job requests and "couldn't hold it in again". **The sun stole** is what he expects of himself: to be someone else's light.
