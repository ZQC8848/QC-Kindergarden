# Lukos (INFJ) character bible

> One line: Doesn't say much, but he's always there when you need him, holding exactly the right bunch of flowers.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Lukos |
| MBTI | INFJ (Advocate / old-soul type) |
| Look | Messy blue-grey yarn hair, worried eyebrows, a very thick scarf |
| Colours | Blue-grey × cream × a little dusty pink |
| Signature props | Chunky blue-grey and cream striped knitted scarf, pink tulips wrapped in kraft paper |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: Crowds drain him. He likes watching from a corner, and he's most relaxed on his own.

**N (Intuitive)**: Looks at the layer underneath. When someone says "I'm fine", he can hear whether it's true. Extremely sensitive to other people's moods.

**F (Feeling)**: Decides by asking "is this good for everyone?" He can swallow his own hurt; he can't watch someone else be hurt.

**J (Judging)**: Inside, there is a very clear order. His quiet isn't a lack of opinions; he's waiting for the right moment to say them. Once decided, he is stubborn.

## Personality in the design

- **The worried, upward-slanting eyebrows**: default expression, "slightly concerned". He isn't worried about anything in particular; he's just always keeping an eye on how everyone is.
- **The scarf up to his chin**: his safety barrier. The thicker the scarf, the more distance he needs. Lending it to someone is how he says "I care about you."
- **Oversized cardigan with wooden buttons**: gentle, worn-in, reassuring.
- **Blue-grey hair and eyes**: the whole palette is an overcast day, but the gentle kind.
- **The pink tulips**: the only bright colour. Underneath, he is soft and rather romantic.

## Habits

- Speaks slowly and quietly, and every sentence has been thought through.
- Remembers things people mention in passing, then brings them out on their birthday.
- Habitually pulls his hands up inside his sleeves.
- Catchphrases: "Are you okay?" "Do you want… me to stay with you?"

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the most recognisable cells are **shy** and **worried**. Happy is a small, closed-mouth smile; he rarely laughs out loud. Angry isn't an outburst, it's eyebrows drawn tighter and silence. Crying is soundless, tears falling with his mouth shut. Proud is the least natural of all; he isn't used to being pleased with himself.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: quiet afternoons, long one-to-one talks, snow, preparing little surprises for people.
- Dislikes: arguments, being praised in public, being forced to choose, seeing anyone left out.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: enormous empathy, reliable, principled. Everyone's secret guardian.
- **Growth points**: so focused on others he forgets himself; can't say no; holds things in until he suddenly breaks.
- **Direction**: say what he needs. Let himself be looked after too.

## Relationships

- He and **Ruanruan** are quiet friends who can sit side by side for an afternoon without a word.
- He is the one who tidies up after **Fufu** and **Haide** make trouble, though he never says anything about it.
- **Mushi**'s enthusiasm is a bit much for him, but he trusts him deeply.
- He quietly watches **Aisha** and thinks she is lonelier than she looks.

## Meaning of the props

**The tulips** aren't for any one person. They are his portable reserve of gentleness: when someone is sad, he hands over a stem. **The scarf** is his own reserve. Wrapped tight, it's for him; unwound and lent out, it's for someone else.
