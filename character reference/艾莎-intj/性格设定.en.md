# Aisha (INTJ) character bible

> One line: Everyone else is playing; she's playing chess. Everyone else is playing chess; she's already three moves ahead.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Aisha |
| MBTI | INTJ (Architect / little-strategist type) |
| Look | Straight crimson hair past the knees, full fringe, a silver chess-knight hairpin, serious straight eyebrows |
| Colours | Crimson × black × silver |
| Signature props | Silver chess-knight hairpin, crimson leather hardback with gilt edges |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: Doesn't need an audience. She does things to get them done, not to be seen doing them.

**N (Intuitive)**: Always asking "and then?" Show her a situation and her mind runs the next moves on its own.

**T (Thinking)**: Cool, objective, no pleasantries. She judges a thing by its result, not by who did it.

**J (Judging)**: There is a plan, and the plan has a backup. She hates variables, but when one appears she is the first to be calm again.

## Personality in the design

- **Red hair down to her knees**: not a strand out of place. Even her hair has order.
- **Straight eyebrows and a closed mouth under the fringe**: default expression, "evaluating". Not angry. Thinking.
- **Military-cut crimson coat with silver studs**: a little authority. She holds herself to a high standard.
- **Black turtleneck, small silver charm on the belt**: restrained overall, particular in the details.
- **The knight hairpin**: in chess the knight moves along the route nobody expects. So does she.

## Habits

- Speaks briefly and precisely, without adjectives. Partly because she has a slight stutter and short sentences catch less.
- Has the plan written before the others have finished arguing.
- Usually has the Red Book in her hand. Nobody knows what's in it.
- Catchphrases: "And then?" "This is more efficient." "I, I told you so."

## Expressions and moods

On her sheet the truest cells are the **level, calm stare** and the **slightly smug raised eyebrow**. Surprise is wide eyes, gone again in a second. Anger is lowered brows and a cold look, no sound. Crying is the least like her; when it happens it's silent tears, and she finds it humiliating. Shy is turning away and letting her hair cover her face.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: chess, the moment a plan works, a quiet study, talking to clever people, being treated as an opponent rather than a child.
- Dislikes: crying without logic, plans changed at the last minute, being patted on the head, "you're too serious".

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: calm, far-sighted, gets things done. The group's brain.
- **Growth points**: too cold; can't express concern; thinks everyone is "too slow"; won't allow herself a mistake; hides the stutter as a flaw, and the hiding makes it worse.
- **Direction**: learn to say "I'm worried about you" instead of "this is inefficient". Accept an imperfect result.

## Relationships

- **Luyao** is her research subject: why is this person always exploding? The Red Book has a page on his eruption patterns, times, triggers, durations, plenty of data, no conclusion yet. They hardly ever work together, but she's secretly learning from him. She tried speaking loudly once, stuck on the first word, and never tried again. She knows he wants to read the Red Book, and that he thinks it's a battle plan. She finds the misunderstanding useful and has not corrected it.
- **Haide** is the only one in the group cleverer than she is. She won't say so, but she quietly takes the problems she can't crack and lets him sniff them. He never jokes about her stutter, and that is why she trusts him. The battle plan for the beating was hers, written in the Red Book under the title "Case 001".
- She and **Dianer** play chess in silence, whole afternoons at a time.
- **Mimi** is the best executor her plans have ever had. Case 001 was Aisha's plan; the bat was Mimi's, and Mimi never once asked why. She dislikes that Mimi speaks before she thinks, but Mimi's page in the Red Book has one word: "Reliable." On the subject of Ruanruan they have an understanding: Mimi handles what can be seen, Aisha handles what can't.
- She has no defence at all against **Fufu**'s affection, whatever "so annoying" she says out loud.
- The Red Book has a page for the transfer student **Liiie**, headed "Under observation", first line: "Stated reason for transfer does not hold." The two are polite to each other, wary of each other, and both know they are being watched.
- She is the only one who ever took the Red Book straight to the Chairman to report Haide, and read for ten minutes. Then **QC** said "a little operation" and Haide bit him for a week. She logged it: "Case 014: escalated to the Chairman. Result: failed. Chairman unreliable." She has also noticed that on the first of every month Haide smells of a different perfume, filed as "monthly anomaly, source unknown". And on days QC visits, Haide has ketchup at the corner of his mouth and QC is carrying one more McDonald's bag than usual. She wrote that down, thought about it, and crossed it out. It is the only crossed-out line in the book.
- The Chairman **QC** has a chapter to himself, including the features and serial number of every companion he has brought, currently up to 017. Verdict: "A variable, not a threat." She once spent a month on a plan; QC, sipping orange juice, pointed out the hole in passing and added "not that I'm dealing with it". She hated that. She fixed the hole.
- She really does care about **Ruanruan**. She secretly records every time Ruanruan is picked on and then deals with it. The Red Book also holds one observation only she has read: "When Ruanruan stares into space, she doesn't look scared. She looks like she's accelerating."

## Extra: A slight stutter

- **Very mild.** In short sentences it's almost inaudible. It catches on the first word only when she's nervous, angry, or about to say something long: "I, I told you so."
- **Her strategy is to say less.** Short sentences, no adjectives, think first, then speak. Half of that is INTJ; half is routing around the words that stick. She turned the stutter into a style, and most people just think she's "blunt".
- **It only catches when she cares.** Cold analysis, no stutter. Being hugged by Fufu, stutter. Wanting to comfort Ruanruan and not knowing how to begin, worst of all. So when she stutters, it means she really cares.
- **She hates it.** She calls it "a variable that shouldn't exist". Finish her sentence for her and you get the cold face instantly.
- **Writing doesn't stutter.** Which is why she prefers the Red Book. A great deal of what she can't say out loud is in there, long and fluent.
- **Haide is the only one who has never joked about it**, and Mushi is the only one who waits quietly for her to finish and has never once passed the stutter on as gossip. She remembers both. She still tells Mushi nothing from the Red Book, because she knows he can't keep it. Mushi knows she won't, and doesn't mind.

## Extra: Aisha's book

- **She always has a book with her.** Crimson leather, gilt edges, expensive-looking, serious-looking. In class, at meals, under her pillow at night. Nobody has seen her open it for anyone. Ask what's inside and she says "plans" and closes it.
- **The theories vary.** Luyao thinks it's battle plans, Dianer thinks it's chess notation, Mushi thinks it's a diary, Fufu thinks it's a joke book ("why else does she smile at it on her own?"). All wrong.
- **The truth: it's a grudge notebook.** More precisely, a Ruanruan grudge notebook. When Aisha herself is picked on she doesn't write it down; she deals with it on the spot. When **Ruanruan** is picked on, she writes. Who, when, what, and how long Ruanruan cried. Nothing left out.
- **Every entry is followed by a revenge plan.** Not the crude "hit back" kind. Detailed, staged, discreet plans that look like nothing to do with her. A shoelace that mysteriously snaps on the morning of the race; a favourite seat that happens to be taken every day that week; a joke that happens to land in silence. Execution takes three days to a month. She is patient.
- **Ruanruan has no idea.** She only knows that Aisha "seems to care about her" and that "people who pick on her have been having bad luck lately". She thinks it's luck.
- **Haide's name appears more than anyone's.** From the skirt-flipping page ("Case 001") through stealing the bunny, hiding the bunny, and putting the bunny up a tree, every incident is logged. He is the only one with a chapter of his own.
- **Haide has half noticed.** His luck keeps failing: a bone he definitely buried is gone, his mat is wet at nap time, his favourite ball rolls into the drain, a banana skin appears exactly where he's chasing Fufu. Every time he thinks "the odds are wrong" and tilts his head at Aisha for a long while. She looks calmly back and turns a page. He can smell it's her and can't prove it. It's the one problem in the group he can't solve, and the thing he admires most about her.
- **She doesn't think of it as holding grudges.** She calls it risk management. Ruanruan is her responsibility, and threats to Ruanruan need to be removed systematically. To her this is rational. Almost gentle.
- **The stutter doesn't exist in the book.** Not one word catches when she writes. All the care, all the worry, every "pick on her again and see what happens" that she can't say out loud goes in long and fluent.
- **The book has another half.** Besides grudges, she records everyone: when Luyao will blow, what Dianer is researching, when Whiskle's plant will flower, that Ruanruan's stare "looks like accelerating". She watches all of them; she just doesn't say. If Ruanruan ever opened the book she would find revenge in the front half and care in the back half, and her own name on every page.
- **The first page has one line.** "If anything happens to me, give this book to Ruanruan."

## Meaning of the props

**The knight hairpin** is how she sees herself: never a straight line, but every move has a purpose. **The Red Book** looks like a planner and is actually the grudges she keeps on Ruanruan's behalf, plus everything she can't say to anyone. Revenge in the front half, observations in the back. Both halves together are the whole Aisha.
