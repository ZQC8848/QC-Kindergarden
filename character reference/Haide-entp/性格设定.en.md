# Haide (ENTP) character bible

> One line: The smartest and most famous one in the group is a dog. And he didn't use to be one.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Haide |
| MBTI | ENTP (Debater / troublemaker type) |
| Look | Husky puppy, four legs, grey-and-white fur, blue eyes, a grin with small fangs showing |
| Species | Currently a dog. Formerly not. |
| Colours | Slate grey × cream × red collar |
| Signature props | Red leather collar, round silver name tag |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Too much energy. Three quiet minutes and he needs something to do, preferably something that gets everyone moving.

**N (Intuitive)**: Sees the other option. Rules exist to be tested, not followed. His brain runs faster than everyone else's, often faster than he can keep up with.

**T (Thinking)**: Argues, and argues well. Tell him "no" and he asks "why"; if you can't answer, he has won. Not very aware of other people's feelings. Never meant to hurt anyone, just didn't think of it.

**P (Perceiving)**: Master of the spur of the moment. What's the plan? The plan is to start running.

## Personality in the design

- **Thick, arched eyebrows**: the most expressive face in the group, and the eyebrows can act on their own.
- **The little fangs**: not aggression. That's the grin that means "I just thought of another bad idea."
- **Bright blue eyes**: curiosity in physical form. If he stares at something for more than three seconds, that thing is in trouble.
- **The red collar and name tag**: the only rule he's willing to wear, because someone gave it to him seriously. His name is on the tag and he is very proud of it.
- **The curled tail with the white tip**: wags like a propeller when he's excited. The most honest mood indicator he has.

## Habits

- Talks in bursts and loves the rhetorical question: "And why can't we…?"
- Favourite game: "Can this be taken apart?"
- Steals other people's things, not because he wants them but to see the reaction.
- Catchphrases: "Just try it!" "I have a better idea."

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the stars are the **smug grin** and the **shocked stare**. Angry is a frown and a pout, "not convinced" rather than real rage. He almost never cries; when he does it's full volume, which means he is truly wronged. Shy is turning his face away while his ears go red.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: new things, the moment he wins an argument, chase games, being called "clever".
- Dislikes: "because that's the rule", doing the same thing twice, being handled like a little kid.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: quick, full of ideas, never afraid of authority, finds the way out of any deadlock.
- **Growth points**: speaks before he thinks and it hurts people; starts everything, finishes little; cares more about winning than about being right.
- **Direction**: think one second about how it will land before opening his mouth. Finish one thing.

## Relationships

- With **Fufu** he is a trouble partnership. Together their IQ doubles and their damage quadruples.
- His favourite person to bait is **Luyao**, because Luyao answers every single line seriously, then explodes and chases him round the courtyard shouting. Haide thinks this is the best game in the world.
- He and **Aisha** are the only two "genuinely clever" people in the group. He wins the arguments, she wins at chess, and neither of them says so. He never jokes about her stutter. It is his one hard line. Also, lately he keeps having bad luck: bones go missing, his mat gets wet, his ball rolls into the drain. The odds feel wrong, he can smell it's her, and he can't find proof. It's the one problem he can't solve.
- He steals **Ruanruan**'s bunny and hides it, watches her cry, then hurries to give it back.

## Extra: A dog, and also the smartest and most famous one here

- **Undisputed top intelligence.** Not "smartest dog". Smartest of everyone. Aisha's plans, Dianer's theories, Luyao's schedules all end up run past him, and one sniff tells him where the hole is.
- **He does not care about this at all.** Being clever is for playing with, not showing off. When someone gasps "a dog came up with that?", he tilts his head: "And?"
- **More famous than the people.** Outsiders might not know the other characters' names, but they've all heard of "the husky that solves problems". He has fans, legends, visitors. His response to visitors is to walk off with one of their shoes.
- **"He's just a dog" is his best joke and his biggest contrast.** He chases his own tail, panics at the vacuum cleaner, then turns round and solves something nobody else can. Given that he used to be a person, the tail thing is even funnier.
- **The loneliness of being clever.** Very few people can follow his thinking, so he makes trouble, because trouble is the one thing that gets everyone involved at once. Aisha and Dianer are among the few who can get him to "talk seriously".

## Meaning of the props

**The red collar** is his link to this group, and the only physical trace of the beating and the vow. He can disobey everything else, but he never loses the collar. Mimi wrote the name on the tag, and it reminds him of two things: what he did, and that someone cares what he's called.
