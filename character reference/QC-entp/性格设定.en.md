# QC (ENTP) character bible

> One line: The kindergarten's Chairman. Shows up a few times a year, always in a Hawaiian shirt, always holding an orange juice, always with a new companion.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | QC |
| MBTI | ENTP (Debater / absentee-boss type) |
| Status | Chairman of the kindergarten (in name), Haide's distant uncle |
| Age | An adult, but chibi-proportioned like everyone here, about the same height as the kids |
| Look | Messy indigo hair, sunglasses pushed down his nose, an open Hawaiian shirt, a shell necklace, sandals, and an orange juice, always |
| Colours | Indigo × white × orange |
| Signature props | Black square sunglasses, silver shell necklace, blue-and-white bead bracelet, iced orange juice with an orange slice, and a gold convertible Ferrari (when it's parked at the gate, the whole kindergarten comes to look) |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: The centre of the party wherever he goes. He doesn't try; the moment he appears the mood changes, the kids crowd in, the teachers frown.

**N (Intuitive)**: A sharp mind, straight to the heart of things. The kindergarten's accounts, staff, problems: one glance and he knows what's wrong. Then he leaves it.

**T (Thinking)**: Argues beautifully, and is especially good at arguing why this particular thing doesn't need doing by him. Not attuned to feelings. When a child cries, his first move is to hand over an orange juice.

**P (Perceiving)**: Life philosophy: let it be. A plan? None. Responsibility? Delegate if at all possible. Deadlines? What are those.

## Personality in the design

- **Sunglasses pushed down the nose, eyes showing above them**: he never wears them properly. Worn properly they'd block the sun; pushed down, they look cool. The whole man in one detail: form over function.
- **The open Hawaiian shirt and board shorts**: this is what he wears to board meetings. Told to "dress for the occasion", he says "this is my formal wear".
- **The ever-present orange juice**: finish one, there's another. Nobody has seen his hand empty. The kids suspect it isn't orange juice. He says "you're too young."
- **The shell necklace and bracelet**: every visit, he's come straight from some beach. The shells on the necklace change now and then, which means the beach changed too.
- **Hands in pockets and a raised eyebrow in his reference image**: default posture, "I'm here, but I'm not planning to work".
- **The tan**: the only skin tone in the group that's different, because he spends three hundred days a year in the sun.

## Habits

- Talks slowly and lazily, fond of "eh", "whatever", "up to you".
- Every visit: one lap of the kindergarten, a pat on every head, then off to find Haide.
- Halfway through a meeting he stands up, says "I'm getting a refill", and doesn't come back.
- Catchphrases: "You guys figure it out." "What's the rush, have a drink first." "Where's my nephew?"

## Extra one: Chairman, and a completely absentee one

- **His name is at the top of every document the kindergarten owns.** Chairman, legal representative, founder: all him. The number of times he actually appears in a year fits on one hand.
- **Who actually runs the place, he doesn't know.** He assumes there's a principal. The principal assumes he's handling it. In practice, it's Mimi and Luyao.
- **Every visit is unannounced.** No warning, he jumps off the school bus in a Hawaiian shirt with an orange juice in hand and a new companion behind him. The teachers scramble, the kids cheer, he waves and says "don't mind me", and then genuinely nobody does.
- **His contribution to the kindergarten is money.** A great deal of it. The PS5 in the game room was an impulse buy of his, "for the kids", which became Haide's private console. He didn't intervene: "a dog playing PS5, that's fine." He never asks where the money goes; he signs. Luyao once tried to walk him through the budget. He looked for three seconds, signed, said "you're doing great", and Luyao's cap went crooked.
- **Not stupid. Just not interested.** He can see exactly where the kindergarten's problems are, and now and then, over an orange juice, he'll point out in passing the hole in a plan Aisha spent a month on. Then he says "not that I'm dealing with it."
- **He's helpless with the children's actual problems.** He can handle contracts, adults, any negotiation, and cannot handle a crying Ruanruan. He's tried reasoning, tried orange juice, tried getting his companion to help. Nothing. His final method is Mimi: "This one's yours."

## Extra two: Haide's distant uncle

- **He and Haide are related, too distantly for anyone to say how.** But one look at the two of them together and you know they're family: same grin, same eyebrows, same "and why not?"
- **When Haide was still a boy, he was the only one in the family who thought "this kid's fun".** Everyone else called Haide a menace; he said "I was like that too". It didn't get Haide shouted at any less, but Haide remembered.
- **He knows about Haide becoming a dog.** His reaction was to laugh for five solid minutes, say "serves him right", and then "he's cute though."
- **He really is looking for a way to turn Haide back.** He has asked people, gone through books, called in favours. It's the one thing he's done on his own initiative in years.
- **The motivation keeps dropping.** Because every time he visits, Haide is thriving: four fast legs, someone bought him a collar, someone wrote his name tag, smartest in the group, most famous in the school, and no trousers required. He crouches to look at Haide, Haide tilts his head back at him, three seconds of eye contact, and he stands up: "Nah. You're fine as you are."
- **Haide's view: whatever.** He's never once pushed his uncle. Occasionally he asks "found it yet?", the uncle says "still looking", Haide says "oh", and runs off after Fufu.
- **It's the most relaxed relationship in the group.** No talking, no explaining. He arrives and Haide comes and rubs against his leg; he leaves and Haide walks him to the gate. That's all. It's the one relationship QC takes seriously.

## Extra three: A different companion every time

- **He never comes alone.** Always a companion, never the same one. The kids all call her "Auntie", and then discover the next visit brings a different Auntie.
- **What the companions have in common: all beautiful, all with time on their hands, none of them sure why they're at a kindergarten.** They usually stand behind him, smile, and get stared at by children.
- **He makes no attempt to hide any of it.** When someone (Luyao) points out that "this isn't appropriate for a Chairman", he says "what's inappropriate? They all love kids." Luyao blows up. QC hands him an orange juice.
- **The kids react in their own ways.** Fufu finds it hilarious and gives every Auntie a nickname. Mimi rolls her eyes. Aisha logs each companion's features in the Red Book, numbered up to 017. Ruanruan thinks every Auntie is beautiful and can't speak. Mushi talks to each one seriously and asks "are you happy today?"
- **He's not a bad man. He just never grew up.** The womanising isn't malice; it's a total allergy to the word "responsibility". He likes good-looking people, good drinks, good places, and nothing that requires him to be in the same spot tomorrow.
- **Once, he came alone.** No companion, no orange juice. He sat down next to Haide and stayed the whole afternoon. Nobody knows what happened that day. The next visit, everything was back to normal. (That was his first visit after the memory event "QC's Nightmare in Three Acts".)

## Expressions and moods

On his sheet the truest cells are the **grin behind the sunglasses** and the **lazy half-open eyes**. Surprised, the sunglasses slip. Angry has basically never been seen; at most a frown and a "forget it". The crying cell is the least likely in the whole group, though he did laugh till he cried on the day Haide turned into a dog, which just about counts. Shy doesn't exist. Proud is frequent, usually right after he's pointed something out in passing.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: the beach, orange juice, good-looking people, fun, Haide, "not my job".
- Dislikes: meetings, any document he isn't signing, "when are you going to take responsibility?", being called "Chairman" (he prefers "QC").

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: clever, generous, never interferes, means the kids no harm. The financial reason this kindergarten exists.
- **Growth points**: takes no responsibility whatsoever; helpless with children's feelings; the search for a way to change Haide back lasts three minutes at a time; has turned "relaxed" into "avoidant"; speaks before thinking, said "little operation" once and got bitten for a week.
- **Direction**: sit through one whole meeting. Ask Haide once, seriously, "do you actually want to change back?" Bring the same companion twice.

## Relationships

- **Haide** is the only person in this kindergarten he truly cares about. See above.
- **Luyao** hates him more than anyone: one of them wants to run the place well, the other owns it and does nothing. Every meeting Luyao blows up, and every time QC hands him an orange juice.
- **Mimi** thinks he's "useless but harmless" and tells him straight, "just don't make it worse." He says "you got it."
- **Aisha** has given him a chapter of his own in the Red Book, including a number for every companion. Her verdict: "a variable, not a threat."
- **Fufu** is his favourite child (after Haide), because she's the only one who thinks he's funny.
- **Mushi** is the only one who tells him, seriously, "you should come and see the kids more." He says "yes" every time, then doesn't come for three months.
- **Ruanruan** hides behind Mimi when she sees him. He tried talking to her once; she cried. Now he walks round her: "can't handle that one."
- **Dianer** talked to him about games once. Afterwards Dianer said "he's actually clever", which is the highest praise Dianer has ever given an adult.
- **Liiie** is the child he finds "interesting". A transfer student who is exactly as polite to the Chairman as to everyone else: to him that means she doesn't care that he's the Chairman, and he finds that very comfortable. He has a feeling she's his kind, both of them wrapped in a layer of something.

## Meaning of the props

**The sunglasses** pushed down his nose are his attitude: I can see everything, and I choose not to look. **The orange juice** is his social tool; handing it over means "don't take me seriously". **The shell necklace** is where he came from: every shell is some beach, some companion, some afternoon he doesn't intend to remember. The one thing he has never swapped is the small shell on his bracelet. Haide picked it up on a beach and gave it to him, back when Haide was a boy.
