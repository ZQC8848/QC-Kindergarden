## When this is superseded

**Edit this record in place; do not delete it and do not write a fresh one that
contradicts it silently.** Change the status line to point at what replaced it, and add:

- what the new decision is
- **why the original reasoning does not apply any more** — usually the reason was sound
  but belonged to a different question, or an assumption above failed its check

A reversal with its original reasoning intact is one of the most useful things in the
repository: it tells the next person that the obvious objection was already considered,
and on what grounds it stopped applying. A reversal that erases the original just looks
like the decision was never made.
