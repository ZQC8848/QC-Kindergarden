# QC taste — storyboards

Scope: breaking a story into shots — which shots, in what order, how each is framed, and how long each beat is held. Not in use yet.  
Status: no rules yet

No human decision about storyboards has been recorded, so this file holds no rules of its own. Do not derive them from the other domains. Rules enter here only through the update protocol, from QC's own seeds, selections, rejections, and revisions of shot breakdowns.

## Until rules exist

A storyboard tells a story as a sequence of images, so the existing rules already reach it:

- what happens, and in what order: the story rules — character-caused escalation, the reaction chain, callbacks, restraint, the altered ending;
- what each frame shows: the image rules — identity before detail, a captured incident rather than a lineup, props that carry the story, temporary people without drawn eyes;
- how corrections are taken: shared rule 16.

Where those rules leave a gap — how long a beat is held, where a cut lands, how much a single frame reveals — is exactly where the first evidence should be collected.
