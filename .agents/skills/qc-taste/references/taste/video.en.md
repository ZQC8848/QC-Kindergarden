# QC taste — video generation

Scope: generated video — motion, timing, camera movement, sound, and the edit. Not in use yet.  
Status: no rules yet

No human decision about video has been recorded, so this file holds no rules of its own. Do not derive them from the other domains. Rules enter here only through the update protocol, from QC's own seeds, selections, rejections, and revisions of video work.

## Until rules exist

A video is a story told in moving images, so the existing rules already reach it:

- what happens, and in what order: the story rules;
- how every frame reads: the image rules, above all identity before detail and the difference between main and temporary people;
- how the story is broken into shots: the storyboard file, once it has rules;
- how corrections are taken: shared rule 16.

Where those rules leave a gap — how the felted-toy world moves, how a delayed reaction is timed on screen, what sound does for a joke — is exactly where the first evidence should be collected.
