# The Gold Ferrari at Midnight

Every so often QC gets a speeding ticket from two or three in the morning. The plate belongs to his gold convertible Ferrari, the location is the highway outside the city, and the speed is a number he wouldn't dare admit to driving himself.

The problem is that at those hours he isn't in the car at all.

The police checked the cameras and told him only: "It is your car, sir." And every time he collects it, there are a few more grey-and-white husky hairs around the driver's seat.

Meanwhile, an absurd rumour started going round town. Someone had seen a dog in sunglasses driving a gold convertible Ferrari flat out down the highway, roof down, windows down, tongue blown sideways by the wind. No photos. Nobody believed it.

<!-- illustration:1|The midnight rumour nobody believed -->

QC crouched down and asked Haide: "Was it you?"

Haide tilted his head, blinked, gave a very long yawn, and ran off after Fufu.

Dianer was the only one who took the question seriously. He measured Haide's forelegs, looked up the pedal distance and the steering ratio, and wrote in his notebook:

"Theoretically feasible. Requires an extremely high IQ."

He glanced up at Haide, and turned the page.

QC paid the tickets, photographed the hairs one by one into an album called "Evidence", and hung the car keys back exactly where they'd always been.
