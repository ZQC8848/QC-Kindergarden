# The Seven-Day Bite

After becoming a dog, Haide behaved for about two weeks. Once he'd got used to four legs, the kindergarten went straight back to its usual chaos.

Aisha finally took the Red Book to the Chairman. She read out the record for a full ten minutes: things carried off, holes dug, rolling on Luyao's cap, Ruanruan's bunny hung up a tree.

QC listened, scratched his head, and looked at Haide, half joking.

"If all else fails, we could get you a little operation? The kind dogs get."

Haide understood, of course. He froze for one second, his eyebrows went up, and he lunged and clamped onto QC's trouser leg.

<!-- illustration:1|The price of one joke -->

The bite lasted seven days. The kindergarten, the car park, the school bus, the hotel corridor: wherever QC went, Haide followed. Trouser legs, sandals, shirt hems, the orange juice cup. Nothing was spared.

On the seventh day QC finally raised both hands. "I was wrong. Never. I swear."

Haide let go and tilted his head at him. The meaning was clear: and?

In the end QC agreed to drive him to a beach party in the gold convertible Ferrari on the last day of every month. Haide found this compensation entirely satisfactory. Aisha, meanwhile, wrote in the Red Book:

<!-- illustration:2|Compensation, last day of every month -->

"Case 014: escalated to the Chairman. Result: failed. Chairman unreliable."
