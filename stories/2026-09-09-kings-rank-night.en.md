# Kings of Rant: Ranking Night

Once a month, at a fixed moment, Kings of Rant locks the server rankings. On the night before, second place, "PointTaken", and third place, "NightRose", always fight until dawn.

"PointTaken" plays cool and exact, like working through a maths problem. That's Dianer.

"NightRose" plays vicious, fast and unreasonable, and is most active at three in the morning. That's Liiie.

Both are ninety percent sure who the other is, and they have never once talked about games. The day after ranking night, Liiie politely calls in sick; Dianer comes to class in his headphones with dark circles under his eyes. When she's back, they look at each other one beat longer than usual.

<!-- illustration:1|Ranking night, second place versus third -->

First place has never belonged to either of them. The ID "NoDogsAllowed" has held it since the day the server opened, and the forums have hundreds of threads guessing who the legend is.

One day Dianer walked past the game room and saw Haide crouched on the sofa pressing a PS5 controller with his paws. This has long since stopped being worth a second look in this kindergarten, but he glanced at the screen anyway.

The ID in the bottom-left corner was "NoDogsAllowed".

Dianer stood still for five seconds. On the screen, Haide had just wiped all five opponents, tail swaying slowly.

<!-- illustration:2|First place, "NoDogsAllowed" -->

That day Dianer thought about it in class, thought about it at lunch, thought about it all the way home. At three in the morning he reached a decision: he had seen nothing.

He didn't tell Liiie. He didn't tell Mushi. He especially didn't tell Mushi.

Some time later, Dianer passed the game room again. Haide didn't turn round. Eyes on the screen, he said one thing:

"Stop watching. You can't learn it."

Dianer walked away fast.
