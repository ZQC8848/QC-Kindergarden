# The Day Haide Became a Dog

Haide used to be a boy. Clever, mouthy, always in trouble, no different from now, except that back then he had two hands, which were very convenient for trouble.

His worst habit was flipping the girls' skirts when they weren't looking, then running off and grinning over his shoulder. Fufu got it. Ruanruan cried a whole afternoon over it. Aisha filled a full page of the Red Book with it. When he went for Mimi a second time, the girls decided to deal with the problem together.

Aisha handled the route. Fufu lured him into the corner. Mimi led, blocking the exit. Ruanruan stood at the very back holding the bunny, only meant to watch, and then found the nerve to kick him once.

When it was over, Mimi planted the bat on the ground and made a Haide with dirt all over his face swear:

"Do it again and you turn into a dog."

<!-- illustration:1|"Do it again and you turn into a dog." -->

Haide swore, and privately thought: what kind of vow is that. The next day he flipped Mimi's skirt again.

After that there was no flash and no smoke. Everyone walked into the classroom in the morning and found a husky puppy sitting on Haide's little stool. The blue eyes, the arched eyebrows and the grin with the small fangs had not changed at all.

Mimi looked at him for three seconds.

"I told you."

<!-- illustration:2|Next morning, one husky on the stool -->

It took Haide only a moment to accept reality. Four legs run faster than two, teeth work better than fists, and nobody could ever make him wear trousers again. His one complaint was that without hands he couldn't flip skirts any more.

The words were barely out before Mimi chased him round the room again.

Later that day, Mimi put the red collar on him and wrote "Haide" carefully on the silver tag. He called it a humiliation. He has never taken it off.
