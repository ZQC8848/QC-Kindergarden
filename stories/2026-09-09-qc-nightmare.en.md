# QC's Nightmare in Three Acts

Not long after Haide became a dog, QC had a nightmare in three acts.

In the first act, Mimi knocked him flat with one swing and demanded, furious: "Like giving my character a baseball bat? See? This is what a bat is for."

<!-- illustration:1|Act one: this is what a bat is for -->

In the second act he thought he'd woken up, and found himself tied to the floor. Whiskle kicked him and wanted to know why he'd been written so pretty that everyone takes him for a girl. Aisha kicked him second and said QC was the one who should have been written with a stutter. Ruanruan kicked him too, as hard as she could, insisting that what she'd wanted was light blue, not pink.

Luyao came last. QC had already shut his eyes and was waiting for the kick, but Luyao stood with his hands behind his back and said: "I'm not fond of the short-temper setup, but the character's pretty cool. I'll let you off."

<!-- illustration:2|Act two: the authors' tribunal -->

In the third act, QC was lying in the middle of a highway north of Los Angeles. A big truck came roaring at him, the driver's seat hidden behind the headlights, and only one bellow got closer and closer:

"I'll f***ing flatten you! YOU'RE the f***ing dog! Come back as a dog next time!"

<!-- illustration:3|Act three: come back as a dog next time -->

QC woke with a start. The sheets were soaked through. He sat in the dark for a long time, without turning on the light, without an orange juice.

Ever since, every time he comes to the kindergarten, there is one extra bag of McDonald's in his hand. He never explains who it's for. He does his lap, walks up to Haide, and the paper bag disappears.

Now and then Aisha notices ketchup at the corner of Haide's mouth. She once wrote it down in the Red Book, thought about it, and crossed it out.
